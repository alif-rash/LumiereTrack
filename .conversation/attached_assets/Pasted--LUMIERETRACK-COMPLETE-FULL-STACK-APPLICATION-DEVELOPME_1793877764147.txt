# LUMIERETRACK – COMPLETE FULL-STACK APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

Build a complete, fully functional, modern web application called **LumiereTrack**.

LumiereTrack is a university group-project management, collaboration and contribution tracking platform designed to improve teamwork, individual accountability, transparency and communication between university students and professors.

This must be a real, working full-stack application, not just a visual prototype.

The platform will allow students to manage multiple university courses, participate in group assignments, divide responsibilities, submit their individual contributions, monitor group progress and receive feedback and marks from their professors.

Professors will have separate accounts and dashboards where they can manage their courses, create group assignments, monitor student contributions, review submissions, provide feedback and grade students.

**IMPORTANT SCOPE RESTRICTION**

LumiereTrack is exclusively designed for GROUP PROJECTS and GROUP ASSIGNMENTS.

Do not build an individual assignment management or individual assignment submission platform.

Students can have individual responsibilities and tasks within a group project, but the overall assignment must belong to a group.

# 18. REQUIRED END-TO-END DEMONSTRATION

The finished application must support the following complete workflow.

**Step 1:** A student registers and logs in.

**Step 2:** The student can view multiple enrolled courses, each with its own professor.

**Step 3:** A professor logs in and creates a group assignment under one of their courses.

**Step 4:** The professor sets the deadline, group size and grading criteria.

**Step 5:** Students create or join a group using an invitation code.

**Step 6:** Group members access the shared project workspace.

**Step 7:** The group creates tasks and assigns responsibilities to individual members.

**Step 8:** A student completes a task and submits their work with an explanation and supporting evidence.

**Step 9:** The contribution appears in the project workspace and activity timeline.

**Step 10:** Other group members can see the shared project update.

**Step 11:** The professor sees the submission in their dashboard.

**Step 12:** The professor reviews the work, adds written feedback and awards marks.

**Step 13:** The professor approves the submission or requests corrections.

**Step 14:** The student receives a notification and can view the feedback and marks.

**Step 15:** The contribution report, project progress and graphs update automatically based on actual records.

**Step 16:** The AI assistant can answer questions about tasks, progress and deadlines using real application data.

Every step must work using separate student and professor accounts.
